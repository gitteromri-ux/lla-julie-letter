One hour, and the full syllabus on its own page

The class is one hour, live on Zoom, taught in six teaching blocks. Priced at seventy-nine dollars.

Why seventy-nine dollars

The masterclass is priced at seventy-nine dollars because our goal is not to earn from the hour. It is to open the top of the funnel: place your method in front of a much wider audience, cover our lead cost, and welcome anyone who wants to go further into the Longevity Blueprint as an informed, warm student. How you will be supported

Julie teaches the hour from a bespoke deck of fifteen to twenty slides that we build together, and iterate together, until every slide is exactly as you would present it. You are never on your own.
