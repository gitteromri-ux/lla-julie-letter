Update two · The masterclass

One hour, and the full syllabus on its own page

The class is one hour, live on Zoom, taught in six teaching blocks. There is no live Q&A: the hour is scripted end to end so every minute earns its place. There is no participant cap: we can host two hundred or more, live and comfortably. The class is priced at seventy-nine dollars, symbolic, as the on-ramp into the full Longevity Blueprint.

the syllabus, in the same format as the blueprint course

The full syllabus lives on its own page, written in the same academic style as the Blueprint eighteen-session curriculum. It names each teaching block, the flow of teaching beats inside it, and the takeaway students carry into their week. You can read it, print it, and mark it up in one sitting.

Session A on Wednesday the sixteenth of September at four o'clock Eastern, and Session B on Sunday the twenty-seventh of September at six o'clock Eastern. If either date does not suit you, two alternatives in the same late-September or early-October window work equally well.

why seventy-nine dollars

The masterclass is priced at seventy-nine dollars because our goal is not to earn from the hour. It is to open the top of the funnel: place your method in front of a much wider audience, cover our lead cost, and welcome anyone who wants to go further into the Longevity Blueprint as an informed, warm student. That is the whole model.

how you will be supported

Julie teaches the hour from a bespoke deck of fifteen to twenty slides that we build together, and iterate together, until every slide is exactly as you would present it. Before the first live class we run a full rehearsal on Zoom, on the same platform and the same flow. Omri from our team is on the call during prep, the rehearsal, and both live sessions. You are never on your own.
